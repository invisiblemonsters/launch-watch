What we read on-chain

  • Not a mixer. The router has no pool and holds no funds. sendEth/sendToken forward each payment straight from the sender to a one-time (stealth) address, then log one ERC-5564 announcement. Amounts and senders stay public, as the site says. So we scored it; mixer-style pools are excluded from Launch Watch.
  • No owner, admin, fee or upgrade path: the verified source has no state variables and no privileged functions, and the EIP-1967 slots are empty.
  • sendToken only pulls tokens from msg.sender. An approval to the router cannot be spent by anyone else.
  • The router was deployed Oct 18, 2026, 1:44 PM PDT, from a new wallet. The domain was registered the same day, and those two facts make up most of the automated score.
  • $COWL has not launched yet (Pons, Oct 19, 2026, 6:00 AM PDT). There is no token contract to score yet.

Open questions for the team

  • The announcement records the requested amount. For fee-on-transfer or rebasing tokens, the stealth address receives less than announced. Does the Inbox show live balances rather than announced amounts?
  • Anyone can post an ERC-5564 announcement directly to the public announcer. Does the Inbox check that an announced payment actually arrived before showing it?
  • Will the $COWL token contract be verified at launch, and will its owner/fee settings be published next to the router on cowl.pro?
