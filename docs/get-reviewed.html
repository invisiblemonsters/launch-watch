What the badge means

What this badge means: On the date shown, Metatron Launch Watch re-ran its automated checks and a person read the verified source code and live owner/admin setup of the contract address(es) listed on this page, pinned to the block (and source commit, if provided) listed here. The findings are on this page with links to public evidence.

What it does not mean: It is not an audit, not a guarantee, not a statement that the project or token is free of risk, and not investment advice. It says nothing about the team's intentions, future behaviour, off-chain systems, other contracts, or market conditions. On-chain state can change after the review; check the date and the current status on this page.

Short form, used under every embedded badge: Reviewed at the listed address and block on the date shown. Not an audit or a guarantee.

Badge states. Current: within 30 days of the review date with no in-scope change detected. Expired: more than 30 days after the review date; the report stays up, marked "Verified as of <date>; not re-checked since". Withdrawn: an in-scope contract changed in a way that adds risk, or the team declined the re-check; the report says so, with the date and reason.

Scope is limited to the addresses listed on the report: the token plus any directly related contracts you name (staking, hook, locker, vesting). Off-chain systems, websites, bridges and third-party protocols are out of scope.
