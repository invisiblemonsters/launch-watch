Robinhood Chain launch security: scores, reviews, and what “not audited” means

Many Robinhood Chain (chain ID 4663) projects ship while contracts are still marked not audited. That is common early on. This page explains what Metatron Launch Watch already publishes for free, how that differs from a full security audit, and how a team can order a Verified badge ($50 BTC) or a 24-hour manual review ($150 BTC) without waiting for outreach.

Short disclaimer: Reviewed at the listed address and block on the date shown. Not an audit or a guarantee. Automated on-chain checks and manual reads of verified source are risk signals, not evidence of wrongdoing, not investment advice, and not a statement that a project or token is free of risk.

If you searched for a Robinhood Chain audit

Search results for “Robinhood Chain audit”, “RH Chain not audited”, or “Robinhood Chain launch security” often mix three different things:

  • A third-party security audit — a firm reads design and code in depth, often over weeks, and publishes a report. Launch Watch does not sell that.
  • Automated launch scoring — what this site does for free: fixed on-chain and hosting checks with evidence links for new Robinhood Chain and Base launches.
  • A dated manual review of listed addresses — what you can order here ($50 Verified badge or $150 24h Review): a person reads verified source and live owner/admin/role setup at a pinned block, with written findings.

If your site or docs already say the contracts are not audited, that statement can stay accurate while you still get a public Launch Watch score page, and optionally a dated badge or review report that links only to the addresses and block that were checked.

What a badge does and does not mean

What this badge means: On the date shown, Metatron Launch Watch re-ran its automated checks and a person read the verified source code and live owner/admin setup of the contract address(es) listed on this page, pinned to the block (and source commit, if provided) listed here. The findings are on this page with links to public evidence.

What it does not mean: It is not an audit, not a guarantee, not a statement that the project or token is free of risk, and not investment advice. It says nothing about the team’s intentions, future behaviour, off-chain systems, other contracts, or market conditions. On-chain state can change after the review; check the date and the current status on this page.

We do not use the words safe, secure, audited, certified, approved, or trusted for Launch Watch badges. Scope is the addresses on the report only.
