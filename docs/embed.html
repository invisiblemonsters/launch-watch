Embed Launch Watch feeds

Use these static feeds to show new scores or high-band launches in your own bots, dashboards, or docs. No API key. Files are regenerated when the site rebuilds.

Wording: These are risk signals, not accusations. Do not label a named project a scam or a rug based only on a Launch Watch score. Higher score = more risk signals under our fixed rules. Not financial advice. Not an audit or a guarantee.

Required attribution

If you embed, republish, or derive a public view from these feeds, include visible credit and a link back:

  • Credit text such as: Scores by Metatron Launch Watch
  • Link to https://invisiblemonsters.github.io/launch-watch/ (or the specific launch page you show)
  • For team-facing CTAs, also link Get reviewed & pricing
