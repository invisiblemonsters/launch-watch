What we read on-chain

  • The addresses above come from the public config of carbonized.app. The repo deliberately keeps its deployment manifests out of git.
  • Scanned Oct 19, 2026. $CARB: 33/100 MODERATE. Drivers: no locked or burned LP, a launch wallet first seen the same day, and a domain registered Oct 19. No mint, pause or blacklist functions; the largest holder has 6.8%. CarbonMarketplace: 42/100 MODERATE. Drivers: unverified source, pause/unpause present, an 18-contract deployer wallet about 1 day old, and the new domain.
  • None of the 9 contracts is upgradeable (no proxy slots set).
  • Role holders, rebuilt from RoleGranted/RoleRevoked events and spot-checked with hasRole: every admin and operating role (DEFAULT_ADMIN, PAUSER, MATCHER, KYC, FEE_MANAGER, WRAPPER, VERIFIER, PRICE_UPDATER, PROJECT_REGISTRAR, ISSUER_ADMIN) sits with one Safe, 0x31fec676ca08ef86a780917d25470b23c0d9219c. The deployer wallet holds no remaining roles, which matches the repo's roles doc.
  • That Safe is currently 1-of-1 (threshold 1, a single owner).
  • NATIVE_ISSUER_ROLE on CarbonCreditNFT is held by a separate wallet, not the Safe. Three credit NFTs exist so far.
  • The marketplace payment token is USDG, 6 decimals (0x5fc5360D0400a0Fd4f2af552ADD042D716F1d168). Staking, issuer bond, governor and timelock addresses are blank in the site config, so we assume they are not deployed yet.

Open questions for the team

  • Could the six unverified contracts (NFT, Marketplace, Registry, Oracle, EmissionVerifier, $CARB) be verified on Sourcify or Blockscout, so users can match the deployed bytecode to the public repo?
  • The admin Safe is 1-of-1 today. Is there a date for adding signers and raising the threshold, and for handing PARAMETER/SLASHER roles to a timelock as the roles doc describes?
  • Which wallet holds NATIVE_ISSUER_ROLE, and what checks gate a native credit issuance?
  • Are the Chainlink Functions consumers (EmissionVerifier, CarbonPriceOracle) still disabled on mainnet, as the docs say they are by default?
  • Is there a published deployment manifest, or a block-explorer list, users can treat as canonical for these addresses?
